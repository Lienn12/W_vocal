# Link Bridge | 🔗 | #38bdf8 | core | A2 | Little words that join ideas: because, although, almost, together.
## The Joining Arch | 🌉 | Two stone halves meet in the middle.
@ Bridge builder | 👷 | One good joint holds everything together.
@ Debate student | 🧑‍🎓 | On the one hand... on the other hand...
@@ Chain link | ⛓️ | Each ring holds the next.
and | /ænd/ | conj | và | I like tea and coffee. | The joining word that adds one thing to another: "salt ... pepper".
but | /bʌt/ | conj | nhưng | I am tired, but I am happy. | The joining word for a contrast: "small ... strong".
or | /ɔːr/ | conj | hoặc | Do you want tea or coffee? | The joining word for a choice between two: "left ... right?"
because | /bɪˈkɔːz/ | conj | bởi vì | I stayed home because it was raining. | The joining word that gives the reason; it answers "why?".
so | /soʊ/ | conj | vì vậy | It was late, so we went home. | The two-letter joining word that gives the result: "It rained, ... we stayed in."
if | /ɪf/ | conj | nếu | If it rains, we will stay home. | The two-letter word that begins a condition.
although | /ɔːlˈðoʊ/ | conj | mặc dù | Although it was cold, we went swimming. | A longer joining word meaning "in spite of the fact that".
however | /haʊˈevər/ | adv | tuy nhiên | It is cheap. However, it is not very good. | A formal contrast word, followed by a comma at the start of a sentence.
unless | /ənˈles/ | conj | trừ khi | I will not go unless you come too. | A joining word meaning "except on the condition that".
while | /waɪl/ | conj | trong khi | She sang while she cooked. | A joining word meaning "during the same period that".
